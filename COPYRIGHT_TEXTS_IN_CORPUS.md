# Copyright Texts in the Research Corpus

These texts are part of our working research corpus but are **NOT hosted in this repository** because they remain under copyright. They are listed here for transparency and for researchers planning future digitization efforts.

**PD Date** = estimated year the work enters the **US public domain** (95 years from publication for works published 1928–1977; life + 70 years for later works). Dates are approximate and subject to legal interpretation.
